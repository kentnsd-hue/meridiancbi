Türkiye Citizenship by Investment Through Real Estate: Your Complete 2026 Guide

October 24, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

By Meridian Advisory | Updated June 2026

Why Türkiye Has Become One of the Most Popular CBI Destinations in the World

Türkiye has quietly emerged as one of the most compelling citizenship-by-investment destinations on the global stage — and in 2026, the case for it is stronger than ever.

Sitting at the crossroads of Europe and Asia, Türkiye offers investors something rare: a powerful passport, a booming real estate market, and a straightforward path to citizenship — all at a price point that undercuts most European programs by a significant margin.

Whether you're an entrepreneur seeking global mobility, a crypto investor looking for a tax-friendly base, or a family wanting a Plan B passport, Türkiye's real estate route deserves a serious look.

Let's break down everything you need to know in 2026.

The Basics: How Türkiye's Real Estate CBI Program Works

Türkiye's Citizenship by Investment program allows foreign nationals to obtain full Turkish citizenship by purchasing real estate valued at a minimum of $400,000 USD.

Here's the framework at a glance:

| Requirement | Details |

|---|---|

| Minimum Investment | $400,000 USD in real estate |

| Holding Period | 3 years (property cannot be sold) |

| Processing Time | Approximately 3–6 months |

| Dependents Included | Spouse and children under 18 |

| Citizenship Type | Full citizenship (not residency) |

| Dual Citizenship | Permitted — no need to renounce existing nationality |

| Physical Residency Requirement | None |

It's worth noting that the investment threshold was raised from $250,000 to $400,000 in June 2022. While there have been discussions about further increases, as of mid-2026, the $400,000 threshold remains in effect — though we always advise clients to act decisively, as CBI thresholds historically only move in one direction: up.

Why Türkiye in 2026? The Strategic Advantages

1. A Genuinely Powerful Passport

The Turkish passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 110+ countries and territories, including Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Thailand, and much of South America and Central Asia. While it doesn't offer automatic EU access, it does provide a strong travel document for business across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and the Americas.

Additionally, Turkish citizens are eligible to apply for a U.S. E-2 Treaty Investor Visa — a critical advantage that programs like St. Kitts and Grenada also share. For entrepreneurs who want to live and operate a business in the United States, this pathway is enormously valuable.

2. A Thriving Real Estate Market

Türkiye's real estate market has been one of the most dynamic in the world over the past several years. Key highlights heading into 2026:

  • Istanbul continues to command strong capital appreciation, particularly in districts like Beyoğlu, Kadıköy, Başakşehir, and the areas surrounding the new Istanbul Airport.
  • Antalya and the Mediterranean coast remain hotspots for rental yield, driven by Türkiye's booming tourism sector (which surpassed 60 million visitors in 2025).
  • Ankara and Izmir are attracting interest from investors looking for lower entry points with strong upside.
  • The combination of a favorable exchange rate and strong domestic demand has created compelling value for foreign buyers purchasing in USD, EUR, or GBP.

Unlike some CBI programs where you're purchasing overpriced "approved" developments, Türkiye allows you to buy any qualifying real estate — residential, commercial, or land — giving you genuine flexibility and market-rate pricing.

3. No Residency Requirement

You do not need to live in Türkiye to obtain or maintain your citizenship. There are no minimum stay requirements, no tax residency obligations triggered by citizenship alone, and no annual reporting mandates simply for holding a Turkish passport.

You invest, you receive citizenship, and you use it as you see fit.

4. Dual Citizenship Friendly

Türkiye fully permits dual (and multiple) citizenship. You will not be asked to renounce your current nationality, and your existing government will not be notified unless your home country's own laws require disclosure.

5. Family Inclusion

Your spouse and children under 18 are included in the application at no additional investment cost. This makes the program particularly attractive for families seeking a collective mobility upgrade.

The Process: Step by Step

Here's what the journey typically looks like when you work with an experienced advisory firm like Meridian:

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Strategy Session

We assess your goals — whether that's global mobility, U.S. E-2 eligibility, asset diversification, or a family safety net — and determine whether Türkiye is the right fit compared to other programs.

Step 2: Property Selection

You identify qualifying real estate that meets the $400,000 minimum. This can be a single property or multiple properties that collectively meet the threshold. We work with vetted partners on the ground in Istanbul, Antalya, and other key markets to ensure you're getting fair value and clean title.

Step 3: Property Purchase & Valuation

The property is purchased and independently appraised. A critical step: the government valuation must confirm the property meets the minimum threshold. Title deed (tapu) transfer is completed.

Step 4: Application Submission

Your citizenship application, along with supporting documentation (passports, background checks, proof of investment, family documents), is submitted to the relevant Turkish authorities.

Step 5: Due Diligence & Review

Turkish authorities conduct background checks and review the application. This stage typically takes 2–4 months.

Step 6: Citizenship Approval & Passport Issuance

Upon approval, you receive your Turkish ID and can apply for your Turkish passport. Total timeline from property purchase to passport in hand: approximately 3–6 months.

What Can You Buy? Real Estate Options

One of Türkiye's biggest advantages is the flexibility of qualifying investments. Unlike programs that restrict you to government-approved developments (often at inflated prices), Türkiye allows:

  • Residential apartments and villas — ideal for personal use or rental income
  • Commercial properties — offices, retail spaces, warehouses
  • Land — for development or long-term hold
  • Multiple properties — you can combine several properties to meet the $400,000 threshold

Where to Invest: Top Markets in 2026

Istanbul — The economic heart of Türkiye. Best for capital appreciation and long-term value. Look at European-side neighborhoods like Beylikdüzü, Esenyurt (for value), or Şişli and Beşiktaş (for premium positioning).

Antalya — The tourism capital. Strong short-term rental yields, particularly in Lara, Konyaaltı, and Kalkan. The Mediterranean lifestyle is a genuine draw for families.

Bodrum & Fethiye — Aegean coast luxury markets attracting international buyers and premium rental returns.

Ankara — The capital city. Lower entry prices, strong institutional demand, and increasing infrastructure investment.

Common Questions We Get About Türkiye CBI

Can I rent out the property during the 3-year holding period?

Yes. You can generate rental income from day one. You simply cannot sell the property for three years.

What happens after 3 years?

The property is yours to sell, hold, or do with as you wish. Your citizenship is permanent and irrevocable — it does not depend on continued property ownership.

Is Turkish citizenship really permanent?

Yes. Once granted, Turkish citizenship is for life. It can be passed down to future children as well.

Do I need to pay taxes in Türkiye?

Turkish citizenship alone does not make you a tax resident. Türkiye taxes based on residency, not citizenship. If you don't live in Türkiye for more than 183 days per year, you're generally not considered a tax resident. However, rental income from Turkish property is subject to local taxation. We always recommend consulting a cross-border tax advisor, and we can connect you with one.

Can I apply from abroad?

The process requires at least one or two brief visits for property purchase and biometric enrollment, but you do not need to relocate. Most of the process can be managed remotely with proper legal representation.

Will there be a price increase?

We cannot predict policy changes with certainty, but the trend across all global CBI programs has been toward higher thresholds and stricter requirements. The Türkiye threshold has already been raised once (from $250K to $400K). If you're considering it, 2026 is the time to move.

✅ Want a tangible real estate asset rather than a donation or fund investment

✅ Need U.S. E-2 Treaty Visa eligibility to live and work in America

✅ Value speed — you want citizenship in months, not years

✅ Are looking for a family-inclusive solution without per-person investment increases

✅ Are interested in portfolio diversification into a high-growth emerging market
