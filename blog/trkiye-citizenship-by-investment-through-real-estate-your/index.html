Türkiye Citizenship by Investment Through Real Estate: Your Complete 2026 Guide

November 24, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

Published by Meridian Advisory | 2026

Why Türkiye Has Become One of the Most Popular CBI Destinations in the World

Türkiye has quietly become one of the most compelling citizenship-by-investment destinations on the global stage. Straddling Europe and Asia, boasting a $1 trillion+ GDP, and offering a pathway to citizenship that is faster and more accessible than most European alternatives, Türkiye's real estate investment route has seen a surge in applications from entrepreneurs, investors, and globally mobile professionals seeking a powerful Plan B.

In 2026, the program remains one of the most attractive options for high-net-worth individuals looking to combine a sound real estate investment with the strategic advantages of a second passport.

Here's everything you need to know.

The Basics: How Türkiye's Real Estate CBI Program Works

Türkiye's Citizenship by Investment program allows foreign nationals to obtain full Turkish citizenship — not just residency — by purchasing real estate valued at a minimum of $400,000 USD.

Key Program Requirements:

| Criteria | Details |

|---|---|

| Minimum Investment | $400,000 USD in real estate |

| Holding Period | 3 years (the property cannot be sold within this period) |

| Eligible Property Types | Residential, commercial, land, or a combination |

| Number of Properties | One or multiple, as long as total value meets the threshold |

| Family Inclusion | Spouse and children under 18 are included |

| Processing Time | Approximately 3–6 months |

| Citizenship Type | Full citizenship with Turkish passport |

| Dual Citizenship | Permitted — no need to renounce existing nationality |

The $400,000 threshold was raised from $250,000 in mid-2022, reflecting the program's growing popularity and Türkiye's desire to attract higher-value investments. As of 2026, this threshold remains in effect, though there is ongoing speculation about potential future increases — making now a strategic time to act.

Why HNW Individuals Are Choosing Türkiye in 2026

1. A Powerful and Improving Passport

The Turkish passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 110+ countries and territories, including Japan, South Korea, Singapore, and much of South America and Central Asia. Türkiye is also a candidate for EU membership, and while accession timelines remain uncertain, any progress on that front would dramatically increase the passport's value.

For investors coming from countries with more restrictive travel documents, the Turkish passport represents a significant upgrade in global mobility.

2. Gateway to the United States: E-2 Treaty Investor Visa

This is arguably Türkiye's biggest differentiator. Türkiye is one of the few CBI-eligible countries that has an E-2 Treaty with the United States. This means Turkish citizens can apply for a U.S. E-2 investor visa, which allows them to:

  • Live and work in the United States
  • Enroll children in U.S. schools
  • Renew the visa indefinitely as long as the business remains operational

For entrepreneurs and investors who want a pathway into the U.S. market without the decade-long EB-5 backlog, this route is a game-changer.

3. Tangible Asset with Real ROI

Unlike donation-based CBI programs (such as those in some Caribbean nations), Türkiye's program requires a real estate investment — meaning you acquire a tangible, appreciating asset.

Türkiye's real estate market, particularly in cities like Istanbul, Antalya, and Ankara, has shown strong growth driven by:

  • Rapid urbanization and infrastructure development
  • Major projects like the Istanbul Canal and new metro expansions
  • A booming tourism sector (Türkiye welcomed 60+ million tourists in recent years)
  • Favorable exchange rates attracting foreign capital

Investors aren't just buying a passport — they're buying into one of the world's most dynamic property markets.

4. Fast Processing Times

While European programs like Portugal's Golden Visa or Malta's citizenship pathway can take 12–36+ months, Türkiye's program typically delivers citizenship in 3 to 6 months. For individuals who need a second citizenship quickly — whether for business continuity, family security, or travel flexibility — this speed is a major advantage.

5. Full Citizenship, Not Just Residency

Many programs offer permanent residency as a first step, with citizenship years down the road. Türkiye grants full citizenship outright. This means voting rights, the ability to pass citizenship to future children, and the full legal protections afforded to Turkish nationals.

6. Strategic Geographic Position

Türkiye's location at the crossroads of Europe, Asia, and the Middle East makes it a natural hub for international business. Istanbul, in particular, has emerged as a regional headquarters city for multinational companies, fintech firms, and trading houses. Holding Turkish citizenship places you at the center of multiple high-growth markets.

The Step-by-Step Process in 2026

Here's what the journey looks like from initial inquiry to passport in hand:

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Strategy

Work with an experienced advisory firm (like Meridian Advisory) to assess your goals, family situation, and investment preferences. Not every property qualifies, and not every city offers the same ROI — expert guidance is essential.

Step 2: Property Selection & Due Diligence

Identify qualifying properties that meet the $400,000 threshold. This includes title deed verification, valuation reports from licensed appraisers, and ensuring the property has not been previously used for another CBI application.

Step 3: Purchase & Valuation Report

Complete the property purchase and obtain a conformity certificate (uygunluk belgesi) confirming the property meets the investment threshold. The valuation must be conducted by a government-authorized appraisal firm.

Step 4: Title Deed Annotation

A notation is placed on the title deed (tapu) confirming that the property cannot be sold for three years. This is a legal safeguard ensuring compliance with the program's holding requirements.

Step 5: Residency Permit Application

Apply for a short-term residency permit. This is a procedural step that precedes the citizenship application.

Step 6: Citizenship Application

Submit the citizenship application to the General Directorate of Civil Registration and Nationality. Required documents typically include:

  • Passport copies (applicant and family members)
  • Title deed with annotation
  • Valuation report
  • Birth and marriage certificates (translated and apostilled)
  • Biometric photos
  • Residency permit

Step 7: Approval & Turkish ID/Passport Issuance

Upon approval, Turkish ID cards (kimlik) and passports are issued for the main applicant and all included family members.

Which Cities Offer the Best Investment Opportunities?

🏙️ Istanbul

The economic capital and by far the most popular choice for CBI investors. Neighborhoods like Beyoğlu, Kadıköy, Başakşehir, and Ataşehir offer strong rental yields and capital appreciation. Istanbul also benefits from massive infrastructure projects and a thriving expat community.

🏖️ Antalya

Türkiye's Mediterranean gem. Popular with tourists and retirees, Antalya offers excellent rental income potential, particularly in the short-term vacation rental market. Property prices are lower than Istanbul, making it possible to acquire larger or more luxurious properties at the $400,000 threshold.

🏛️ Ankara

The capital city offers more affordable property and strong demand from government employees and university students. A less glamorous but pragmatically solid choice for investors focused on steady rental income.

🌊 Bodrum & Muğla

The Turkish Riviera. High-end villa markets here attract luxury buyers, and the area has seen consistent demand from European and Middle Eastern investors.

🏗️ Mersin & Gaziantep

Emerging markets offering lower entry points and high growth potential, particularly as Türkiye's southeastern economy continues to develop.

Important Considerations & Risks

No investment program is without nuance. Here are key factors to weigh:

Currency Risk

The Turkish lira has experienced significant volatility in recent years. Since the investment threshold is denominated in USD, your property's lira-denominated value may fluctuate. However, many developers now price premium properties in USD or EUR, mitigating this risk.

Property Valuation Accuracy

The government requires a formal appraisal, but it is crucial to work with an independent advisor to ensure you are not overpaying. Some developers inflate prices for foreign CBI buyers. This is exactly why working with an experienced consultancy matters.

Three-Year Holding Period

You cannot sell the property for three years. Make sure your investment thesis accounts for this lock-up. If rental income is part of your strategy, factor in property management and occupancy rates.

Tax Implications

Türkiye has property taxes, rental income taxes, and capital gains taxes. Depending on your home country's tax treaties with Türkiye, you may also face obligations abroad. Consult a cross-border tax advisor as part of your planning process.

Potential Threshold Increases

The jump from $250,000 to $400,000 happened in 2022. There is ongoing discussion in Turkish policy circles about further increases. While nothing is confirmed, applicants in 2026 should be aware that the current threshold may not remain indefinitely.
