Dominica CBI: The Most Affordable Path to Caribbean Citizenship in 2026

November 19, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

Why thousands of globally minded investors are turning to the "Nature Isle" for their second passport — and why the value proposition has never been stronger.

The Bottom Line, Upfront

If you've been exploring citizenship by investment (CBI) but assumed it required a seven-figure commitment, Dominica's program deserves your immediate attention. With a minimum investment starting at $100,000 for a single applicant through the Economic Diversification Fund (EDF), Dominica consistently ranks as the most cost-effective Caribbean CBI option — without compromising on passport strength, program integrity, or processing efficiency.

In a landscape where competing programs have raised thresholds and tightened quotas, Dominica remains remarkably accessible. Here's everything you need to know in 2026.

A Brief History of Dominica's CBI Program

Established in 1993, Dominica's Citizenship by Investment Programme is one of the longest-running programs of its kind in the world. That longevity isn't accidental — it reflects decades of stable governance, rigorous due diligence, and a genuine commitment to using CBI funds for national development.

The program has funded hospitals, schools, climate-resilient housing, and geothermal energy infrastructure across the island. For investors, this translates into something critical: legitimacy. Dominica's program has consistently earned top marks from independent assessments, including the CBI Index published by the Financial Times' Professional Wealth Management.

Investment Options in 2026

Dominica offers two primary routes to citizenship:

1. Economic Diversification Fund (EDF) — The Most Popular Route

| Applicant Type | Contribution Amount |

|---|---|

| Single applicant | $100,000 |

| Applicant + spouse | $150,000 |

| Family of four | $175,000 |

| Additional dependents (under 18) | $25,000 each |

| Additional dependents (18+) | $50,000 each |

The EDF is a one-time, non-refundable government contribution. It's straightforward, fast, and requires no property management or ongoing obligations.

2. Real Estate Investment

  • Minimum investment: $200,000 in a government-approved real estate project
  • Property must be held for a minimum of three years (some projects require five)
  • Can be resold to a subsequent CBI applicant after the holding period
  • Additional government fees apply on top of the real estate purchase price

For most applicants seeking pure affordability and simplicity, the EDF route wins. There's no property to manage, no resale risk, and the total cost is significantly lower.

What Makes Dominica Stand Out in 2026?

🌍 Visa-Free Travel to 140+ Destinations

A Dominican passport grants visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 140+ countries and territories, including:

  • The entire Schengen Zone (27 European countries)
  • United Kingdom
  • Singapore, Hong Kong, and China
  • Brazil, South Africa, and most of Latin America
  • Russia (a distinction few Caribbean passports offer)

For entrepreneurs and investors managing global operations, this eliminates one of the biggest friction points in international business: the visa queue.

💰 Unmatched Affordability

Let's put Dominica's pricing in context against other Caribbean CBI programs in 2026:

| Program | Minimum Investment (Single Applicant) |

|---|---|

| Dominica | $100,000 (EDF) |

| St. Kitts & Nevis | $250,000 (Sustainable Island State Contribution) |

| Grenada | $235,000 (National Transformation Fund) |

| Antigua & Barbuda | $230,000 (National Development Fund) |

Dominica isn't just the most affordable — it's affordable by a significant margin. For families, the savings multiply further.

⏱️ Fast Processing Times

Typical processing time: 3 to 6 months from submission of a complete application. In many cases, applicants receive approval within 90 days. There is no residency requirement — you don't need to visit Dominica before, during, or after the process (though the island is absolutely worth a visit).

🔒 No Residency or Physical Presence Requirements

Unlike Portugal's Golden Visa (which requires periodic stays) or Malta's program (which has residency components), Dominica places zero residency obligations on new citizens. Your citizenship is for life, inheritable, and entirely independent of where you choose to live.

🛡️ Strong Due Diligence = Strong Reputation

Dominica has invested heavily in vetting applicants. The program employs multi-tier due diligence, including checks by international agencies. This protects the program's integrity — and by extension, the value of your passport. A CBI passport is only as strong as the program's reputation, and Dominica's is among the best.

Tax Advantages Worth Noting

Dominica operates a territorial tax system. Citizens are only taxed on income earned within Dominica. There is:

  • No capital gains tax
  • No inheritance tax
  • No wealth tax
  • No foreign income tax (for non-residents)

For crypto investors, digital entrepreneurs, and anyone with a globally distributed income, this structure offers meaningful planning opportunities — especially when paired with professional tax advisory.

Common Questions We Hear

"Will I need to renounce my current citizenship?"

No. Dominica fully allows dual citizenship. Your existing nationality is unaffected.

"Can I include my parents or siblings?"

Yes. The program allows inclusion of a spouse, children, parents, grandparents, and siblings (subject to age and dependency requirements). This is one of the most generous dependent policies among CBI programs.

"Is Dominica the same as the Dominican Republic?"

No — this is a common confusion. The Commonwealth of Dominica is a small, independent island nation in the Eastern Caribbean. The Dominican Republic is a separate, much larger country sharing the island of Hispaniola with Haiti.

"Is the passport respected at international borders?"

Absolutely. Dominica is a member of the Commonwealth of Nations, the United Nations, and CARICOM. Its passport is widely recognized and accepted globally.

Dominica's CBI program makes that asset available at a price point that most other programs simply can't match.
