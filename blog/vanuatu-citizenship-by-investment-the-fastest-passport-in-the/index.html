What Is the Vanuatu Citizenship by Investment Program?

Vanuatu, an archipelago of 83 islands in the South Pacific, launched its Development Support Program (DSP) in 2017. The program grants full, irrevocable citizenship to qualified applicants in exchange for a non-refundable economic contribution to the country's development fund.

Unlike residency-by-investment programs that require years of physical presence before naturalization, Vanuatu's DSP is a direct citizenship program — meaning you receive a passport, not just a residence permit.

The program is legally codified under the Citizenship Act [CAP 112] and regulated by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC) and the Citizenship Commission.

Visa-Free Access: Where Can a Vanuatu Passport Take You?

A Vanuatu passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 90+ countries and territories, including:

  • United Kingdom (6-month visa-free stay)
  • Russia
  • Hong Kong
  • Singapore (30 days)
  • All EU Schengen Zone countries (90 days within 180-day period)
  • Most of the Caribbean and Pacific Island nations
  • Turkey, Georgia, and several CIS nations

The inclusion of Schengen Zone access and UK entry makes Vanuatu one of the most powerful non-Caribbean CBI passports for global travelers and business operators working across European and Asian markets.

The Application Process: Step by Step

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Eligibility Assessment

Work with an authorized agent to verify your eligibility. Key requirements include being over 18, having a clean criminal record, demonstrating lawful source of funds, and meeting health standards.

Step 2: Document Preparation

Compile the required documentation, including certified copies of passports, birth certificates, police clearance certificates, bank references, proof of address, and source of funds declarations.

Step 3: Application Submission

Your authorized agent submits the full application package to the Vanuatu Citizenship Commission along with the required due diligence fees.

Step 4: Due Diligence & Background Checks

Vanuatu conducts thorough international background checks through reputable third-party due diligence firms. Despite the fast processing time, the vetting process is rigorous — this is not a "rubber stamp" program.

Step 5: Approval & Contribution Payment

Upon approval-in-principle, you make the required economic contribution to the development fund.

Step 6: Citizenship Certificate & Passport Issuance

You receive your Certificate of Citizenship and Vanuatu passport. Some applicants have received their passport in as few as 30 calendar days from submission.

Step 7: Oath of Allegiance

Applicants take an oath of allegiance, which can typically be completed at a Vanuatu embassy, consulate, or authorized location — no travel to Vanuatu required.

Common Concerns — Addressed Honestly

"Is Vanuatu citizenship legitimate?"

Yes. The DSP is enshrined in Vanuatu's national legislation and recognized internationally. Vanuatu is a sovereign nation, a member of the United Nations, the Commonwealth of Nations, and numerous international bodies. Passports issued under the DSP are standard Vanuatu passports — indistinguishable from those issued to born citizens.

"Is the due diligence process rigorous enough?"

Vanuatu has significantly strengthened its due diligence protocols in recent years, partnering with international compliance firms and rejecting applicants who fail background checks. The program's credibility depends on maintaining high standards, and the government understands this.

"Will I have to pay taxes to Vanuatu?"

Vanuatu has no personal income tax, no corporate tax on foreign income, no capital gains tax, no estate tax, and no withholding tax. You should, however, consult with a qualified international tax advisor regarding your obligations in your country of primary residence or any other jurisdictions where you have tax ties.

"Do I need to live in Vanuatu?"

No. There is zero physical residency requirement — not before, during, or after the application process.

"Can my family be included?"

Yes. Spouses, dependent children (typically under 25), and dependent parents (typically over 50) can be included in a single application.
