Vanuatu Citizenship by Investment: The Fastest Passport in the World (2026 Guide)

July 29, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

Reading time: 8 minutes

There's a reason Vanuatu has become one of the most talked-about citizenship by investment programs in the world: you can hold a second passport in as little as 30–60 days.

No other sovereign nation on earth processes citizenship this fast. Not St. Kitts. Not Malta. Not Portugal. Vanuatu stands alone in the speed category — and in 2026, the program is drawing more attention than ever from entrepreneurs, crypto investors, and globally mobile professionals who understand that optionality isn't a luxury. It's a necessity.

But speed is only part of the story. Let's break down everything you need to know about Vanuatu's Development Support Program (DSP), what it offers, what it doesn't, and whether it's the right fit for your global strategy.

What Is the Vanuatu DSP?

The Development Support Program (DSP) is Vanuatu's official citizenship by investment pathway, administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). Launched in 2017, it allows qualified applicants to obtain full, legal citizenship and a Vanuatu passport through a direct financial contribution to the country's development fund.

This is not a residency program. It is direct, irrevocable citizenship — no residency requirement, no language test, no interview, no relocation needed.

Why Vanuatu? The Core Advantages

1. Unmatched Processing Speed

Most CBI programs take 3–6 months at minimum. Malta can take over a year. Vanuatu routinely processes applications in 30 to 45 days, with some cases completing in under 30.

For individuals navigating time-sensitive situations — business restructuring, geopolitical instability, tax planning deadlines, or simply the desire to move quickly — this speed is a genuine competitive advantage.

2. Zero Tax Jurisdiction

Vanuatu levies:

  • No personal income tax
  • No corporate income tax
  • No capital gains tax
  • No inheritance tax
  • No withholding tax

For crypto investors, digital entrepreneurs, and those with globally distributed income streams, Vanuatu's tax framework is among the most favorable in the world. While tax residency and obligation depend on your full jurisdictional picture (and professional tax advice is essential), holding citizenship in a zero-tax nation creates significant strategic flexibility.

3. Visa-Free Travel Access

A Vanuatu passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to approximately 100+ destinations, including:

  • 🇬🇧 United Kingdom (up to 6 months)
  • 🇪🇺 Schengen Area (90 days in a 180-day period)
  • 🇭🇰 Hong Kong
  • 🇸🇬 Singapore
  • 🇷🇺 Russia
  • 🇲🇾 Malaysia

The Schengen and UK access alone make this passport remarkably powerful for business travelers and investors who need to move across Europe without the friction of visa applications.

4. No Physical Presence Required

You are never required to visit Vanuatu — not during the application process and not after citizenship is granted. The entire process can be handled remotely through an authorized agent.

There is no residency requirement. No minimum stay. No domicile obligation. You receive citizenship, your passport is delivered, and you continue your life wherever you choose.

5. Dual Citizenship Recognized

Vanuatu fully recognizes dual (and multiple) citizenship. Your existing nationality is unaffected. You do not need to renounce anything. This is a purely additive asset in your global portfolio.

Investment Requirements (2026)

The Vanuatu DSP operates on a non-refundable government contribution model. Here's the current investment structure:

| Applicant Type | Contribution Amount (USD) |

|---|---|

| Single applicant | ~$130,000 |

| Couple | ~$150,000 |

| Family of 4 | ~$180,000 |

Additional costs typically include:

  • Due diligence fees
  • Government processing fees
  • Legal and advisory fees

All in, a single applicant should budget approximately $150,000–$160,000 for the complete process.

The Application Process: Step by Step

Step 1: Initial Consultation & Eligibility Assessment

We evaluate your background, objectives, and eligibility. Not everyone qualifies — Vanuatu conducts legitimate due diligence, and we pre-screen to ensure a smooth process.

Step 2: Document Collection

Standard documentation includes passport copies, police clearances, proof of funds, medical certificates, and a brief personal statement.

Step 3: Application Submission

Your application is submitted to the VFSC along with all supporting documents and the required government fees.

Step 4: Due Diligence

Vanuatu's compliance team conducts background checks through international databases. This typically takes 2–3 weeks.

Step 5: Approval & Oath of Allegiance

Upon approval, you take an oath of allegiance. This can often be done remotely through a Vanuatu consulate or honorary consul in your region.

Step 6: Passport Issuance

Your Vanuatu passport is issued and delivered. The entire timeline from submission to passport in hand: typically 30–60 days.

Common Questions About Vanuatu CBI

Is Vanuatu citizenship internationally recognized?

Yes. Vanuatu is a sovereign nation, a member of the United Nations, the Commonwealth of Nations, and numerous international organizations. The passport is a legitimate travel document accepted worldwide.

Will my current country know I obtained Vanuatu citizenship?

Vanuatu does not proactively notify other governments. However, your obligations regarding disclosure depend on the laws of your home country. We always recommend full compliance with your domestic reporting requirements.

Can I open bank accounts with a Vanuatu passport?

Yes, though banking access depends on the institution. Vanuatu citizenship is generally well-received by international banks, particularly in Asia-Pacific, the Caribbean, and parts of Europe. We can advise on banking strategy as part of your overall plan.

Is Vanuatu on any EU or OECD blacklists?

Vanuatu has faced scrutiny from the EU in the past regarding its CBI program. The government has responded by strengthening due diligence standards. As of 2026, it's important to work with an experienced advisory firm that stays current on any regulatory developments. This is an area we monitor closely at Meridian.

Can I include my children and parents?

Yes. The DSP allows inclusion of a spouse, dependent children (typically under 25 if in full-time education), and in some cases, dependent parents over 50.

Vanuatu isn't for everyone. If your primary goal is the most powerful passport ranking or EU residency rights, there are stronger options. But if you value speed, tax efficiency, zero physical presence requirements, and a legitimate second citizenship at a competitive price point, Vanuatu's DSP is arguably the most compelling program available in 2026.

And no program on earth gets you there faster than Vanuatu.
