Antigua and Barbuda CBI: Why It's the Best Citizenship by Investment Program for Large Families in 2026

November 05, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

For families seeking a second passport without leaving anyone behind, Antigua and Barbuda offers something no other Caribbean program can match.

The Family-First Program

When high-net-worth individuals explore Citizenship by Investment, the conversation almost always starts with investment thresholds and passport strength. But for families with four or more members, one critical factor often gets overlooked: the total cost per dependent.

This is where Antigua and Barbuda separates itself from the pack. While most Caribbean CBI programs charge escalating fees for each additional family member, Antigua and Barbuda was deliberately designed to reward larger families — not penalize them.

If you're a business owner, investor, or entrepreneur with a spouse, children, parents, and even siblings to consider, this program deserves your full attention.

How the Antigua and Barbuda CBI Program Works

Launched in 2013, Antigua and Barbuda's CBI program offers multiple investment routes to full citizenship. The government has refined the program over the years, and as of 2026, it remains one of the most competitive and family-inclusive options in the Caribbean.

Investment Options

1. National Development Fund (NDF) Contribution

  • $230,000 USD for a family of four or more (a significant discount compared to smaller applicant groups)
  • $130,000 USD for a single applicant
  • $150,000 USD for a family of up to three

This is the key number. For families of four or more, the per-person cost drops dramatically — making it the most cost-effective program in the Caribbean at scale.

2. Real Estate Investment

  • Minimum $300,000 USD in a government-approved real estate project (can be held jointly by two applicants, effectively $150,000 each)
  • Property must be held for a minimum of five years
  • Resale value provides a tangible asset, unlike a donation

3. Business Investment

  • Minimum $1,500,000 USD into an approved business venture
  • Or a joint investment of at least $400,000 USD per person with a minimum total of $5,000,000 USD
  • Best suited for entrepreneurial applicants who want operational involvement

4. University of the West Indies (UWI) Fund

  • $260,000 USD contribution for a family of six or more
  • Includes a one-year scholarship for one family member at UWI — a unique educational benefit not available in any other CBI program

While Dominica may appear competitive on headline numbers, Antigua's flat pricing structure for families of four or more, combined with its broader dependent eligibility, often makes it the clear winner when you factor in the full picture.

Who Qualifies as a Dependent?

This is another area where Antigua and Barbuda outperforms. The program allows an exceptionally broad definition of "family":

  • ✅ Spouse
  • ✅ Children under 30 (including those enrolled in higher education)
  • ✅ Parents and grandparents aged 55+ of the main applicant or spouse
  • ✅ Siblings of the main applicant — unmarried, childless, and under 30

That last point is a game-changer. Very few CBI programs include siblings. For applicants who are responsible for extended family members, or for parents applying with adult children and their own elderly parents, Antigua provides a single, unified pathway.

A three-generation application — grandparents, parents, and children — is not only possible, it's common.

Passport Strength and Global Mobility

An Antigua and Barbuda passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to approximately 150+ destinations as of 2026, including:

  • 🇬🇧 United Kingdom — visa-free for up to 6 months
  • 🇪🇺 Schengen Area (all 27 EU countries) — visa-free for 90 days in any 180-day period
  • 🇭🇰 Hong Kong — visa-free
  • 🇸🇬 Singapore — visa-free
  • 🇧🇷 Brazil — visa-free
  • 🇮🇳 India — e-visa available

For families with global business interests, children studying abroad, or parents who want flexible travel in retirement, this level of access transforms daily life.

Processing Timeline

Antigua and Barbuda has invested in streamlining its CBI processing:

  • Standard processing: 3–6 months from submission to approval
  • Accelerated processing: Available for an additional fee, reducing timelines to as little as 45–60 days in some cases

All family members are processed together in a single application, which avoids the delays that come with filing multiple separate cases.

The Five-Day Residency Requirement

Antigua and Barbuda requires new citizens to spend a minimum of five days in the country within the first five years of obtaining citizenship. This is one of the lightest physical presence requirements of any CBI program globally.

For busy families, this is practical. Five days over five years. Most families treat it as a Caribbean vacation and fulfill the requirement effortlessly.

It's worth noting that failure to meet this requirement could affect passport renewal, so while it's minimal, it should be planned for.

Tax Advantages

Antigua and Barbuda does not impose:

  • ❌ Income tax on worldwide income
  • ❌ Capital gains tax
  • ❌ Inheritance tax
  • ❌ Wealth tax

For entrepreneurs, crypto investors, and families with diversified global assets, this tax environment — combined with a second citizenship — provides powerful structuring opportunities. As always, applicants should consult with qualified international tax advisors to understand their obligations in their country of primary residence.

Real-World Scenario: The Case for Antigua

Consider this profile:

A 48-year-old tech entrepreneur based in the Middle East. Married, with four children (ages 8, 12, 17, and 24). His mother, aged 72, lives with the family. His younger brother, aged 27 and unmarried, works in the family business.

That's eight people.

Under Antigua and Barbuda's CBI program, all eight can be included in a single application. The NDF contribution remains at $230,000 — the same flat rate as a family of four.

Under most other Caribbean programs, the brother would be excluded entirely. The 24-year-old might face additional scrutiny or fees. The grandmother might require a separate application.

Antigua handles all of them. One application. One timeline. One investment.

What to Watch in 2026

The Caribbean CBI landscape is evolving. Regional governments have been under increasing international pressure to strengthen due diligence, raise minimum investment thresholds, and align with OECD transparency standards.

Antigua and Barbuda has responded proactively:

  • Enhanced due diligence procedures implemented in coordination with international compliance partners
  • Investment thresholds have been adjusted in recent years and could shift again
  • Bilateral agreements continue to expand passport strength

The window for current pricing and family-inclusive terms is open — but it won't stay open indefinitely. Programs across the Caribbean have historically raised their thresholds with limited advance notice.
