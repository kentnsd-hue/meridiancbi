Second Citizenship for Your Entire Family: Which CBI Programs Let You Include Parents, Siblings, and Beyond?

October 15, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

By Meridian Advisory | June 2026

When high-net-worth individuals explore citizenship by investment, the conversation almost always starts with one question:

"Can my family come with me?"

The answer is yes — but which family members qualify depends entirely on which program you choose. And the differences are more significant than most people realize.

While nearly every CBI program allows you to include a spouse and minor children, the real differentiator lies in how each country treats parents, adult children, siblings, and even grandparents. For families that prioritize keeping multiple generations under one passport, this distinction can — and should — shape the entire strategy.

Let's break it down.

Understanding "Dependents" in CBI Programs

Every citizenship by investment program defines eligible dependents differently. The core applicant (the main investor) always qualifies, but beyond that, each nation draws its own lines around who counts as family.

The typical tiers look like this:

  • Tier 1 (Universal): Spouse and minor children (under 18)
  • Tier 2 (Common): Adult children up to a certain age (usually enrolled in higher education)
  • Tier 3 (Selective): Parents and grandparents above a certain age
  • Tier 4 (Rare): Siblings, unmarried siblings, or extended family

Let's look at the most family-inclusive programs available in 2026.

🇰🇳 St. Kitts & Nevis: The Gold Standard for Family Inclusion

Minimum Investment: USD $250,000 (real estate) or USD $250,000 (Sustainable Island State Contribution — SISC)

St. Kitts and Nevis has long been the flagship of Caribbean CBI — and for good reason. It also happens to be one of the most generous programs when it comes to family dependents.

Who Can Be Included:

  • ✅ Spouse
  • ✅ Children under 18
  • ✅ Children aged 18–30 (if enrolled in higher education and financially dependent)
  • ✅ Parents and grandparents aged 55+
  • ✅ Siblings of the main applicant — unmarried, childless, and under 30

That last point is critical. St. Kitts is one of the very few programs globally that allows siblings to be added to a single application. For families with younger brothers or sisters who haven't yet established independent financial standing, this is an extraordinary benefit.

Processing Time:

Approximately 3–4 months, with an accelerated option available in as little as 45–60 days.

Why Families Choose St. Kitts:

  • Visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 150+ countries
  • No residency requirements
  • No interview required
  • Multi-generational inclusion under one application
  • Established program with a track record dating back to 1984

🇬🇩 Grenada: Family-Friendly With a Unique U.S. Advantage

Minimum Investment: USD $235,000 (real estate) or USD $235,000 (National Transformation Fund)

Grenada is often recommended for families with ties to the United States — and for good reason. It's the only Caribbean CBI nation with an E-2 Treaty Investor Visa agreement with the U.S., meaning Grenadian citizens can apply to live and work in America through the E-2 route.

Who Can Be Included:

  • ✅ Spouse
  • ✅ Children under 18
  • ✅ Children aged 18–30 (if in full-time education and financially supported)
  • ✅ Parents and grandparents aged 55+
  • ✅ Siblings of the main applicant — unmarried, childless, aged 18+

Like St. Kitts, Grenada stands out for sibling inclusion. This makes it one of the most family-comprehensive programs in the world.

Processing Time:

Approximately 4–6 months.

Why Families Choose Grenada:

  • E-2 visa pathway to the United States
  • Sibling and parent inclusion
  • No residency requirement
  • Visa-free access to 145+ countries, including China and the Schengen Area
  • Strong due diligence reputation

🇦🇬 Antigua & Barbuda: Built Around the Family Unit

Minimum Investment: USD $230,000 (real estate) or USD $230,000 (National Development Fund)

Antigua and Barbuda has explicitly positioned itself as a family-first CBI program. The government has historically marketed the program around multi-generational applications.

Who Can Be Included:

  • ✅ Spouse
  • ✅ Children under 18
  • ✅ Dependent children up to age 30
  • ✅ Parents and grandparents aged 55+
  • ✅ Siblings aged 18–30 (unmarried, without children, and financially dependent)

Unique Requirement:

Antigua requires that at least one family member spends a minimum of 5 days in the country during the first 5 years of holding citizenship. It's minimal, but worth noting.

Processing Time:

Approximately 3–5 months.

Why Families Choose Antigua:

  • One of the broadest dependent definitions in the Caribbean
  • Beautiful country with growing infrastructure for relocating families
  • Visa-free access to 150+ countries
  • University of the West Indies campus for families prioritizing education

🇲🇹 Malta: The European Option for Families

Minimum Investment: Starting from approximately €690,000+ (combination of contribution, property, and donation)

Malta's Citizenship by Naturalisation for Exceptional Services by Direct Investment (commonly known as the Malta CBI program) is the premier European option — and it allows a solid range of family members.

Who Can Be Included:

  • ✅ Spouse or partner in a long-term relationship
  • ✅ Children under 18
  • ✅ Adult children up to age 29 (if unmarried, financially dependent)
  • ✅ Parents and grandparents aged 55+ (if financially dependent)

Who Cannot Be Included:

  • ❌ Siblings are not eligible under the current Malta program

Processing Time:

12–36 months. Malta's process is longer due to a mandatory residency period (12 months for the higher contribution tier, 36 months for the lower tier).

Why Families Choose Malta:

  • Full EU citizenship — live, work, and study anywhere in the European Union
  • Passport ranked among the top 10 globally
  • World-class healthcare and education systems
  • Ideal for families wanting a permanent European base

🇵🇹 Portugal Golden Visa: Residency First, Citizenship Later

Minimum Investment: Starting from €250,000+ (investment funds; real estate in major cities is no longer eligible as of 2023 reforms)

Portugal's Golden Visa is technically a residency-by-investment program, not a direct CBI. However, it provides a clear pathway to full Portuguese (and EU) citizenship after approximately 5 years, making it one of the most sought-after programs globally.

Who Can Be Included:

  • ✅ Spouse or registered partner
  • ✅ Minor children
  • ✅ Dependent children over 18 (if enrolled in education)
  • ✅ Parents of the main applicant or spouse (if aged 55+ or dependent)

Who Cannot Be Included:

  • ❌ Siblings are not eligible

Processing Time:

Residency approval in approximately 6–12 months. Citizenship eligibility after 5 years with minimal physical presence requirements.

Why Families Choose Portugal:

  • Path to EU citizenship and passport
  • Extremely low physical presence requirement (just 7 days per year on average)
  • NHR (Non-Habitual Resident) tax benefits for qualifying applicants
  • High quality of life, excellent education, and strong healthcare

Quick Comparison: Family Inclusion at a Glance

| Program | Spouse | Minor Children | Adult Children | Parents/Grandparents | Siblings |

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| St. Kitts & Nevis | ✅ | ✅ | Up to 30 | 55+ | ✅ (under 30) |

| Grenada | ✅ | ✅ | Up to 30 | 55+ | ✅ (18+) |

| Antigua & Barbuda | ✅ | ✅ | Up to 30 | 55+ | ✅ (18–30) |

| Malta | ✅ | ✅ | Up to 29 | 55+ | ❌ |

| Portugal | ✅ | ✅ | If dependent | 55+ (if dependent) | ❌ |

So Which Program Is Right for Your Family?

The "best" program doesn't exist in a vacuum. It depends on your family's unique composition, goals, and priorities:

  • Have younger siblings you want to include? → St. Kitts, Grenada, or Antigua should be at the top of your list.
  • Need a pathway into the United States? → Grenada's E-2 treaty makes it the clear frontrunner.
  • Want full EU citizenship for your entire family? → Malta offers the most direct route, with Portugal as a longer-term alternative.
  • Prioritize speed and simplicity? → St. Kitts' accelerated processing is hard to beat.
  • Have elderly parents you want to protect? → Nearly all programs accommodate parents 55+, but verify financial dependency requirements.

The critical mistake we see families make is choosing based on investment cost alone. A program that saves $50,000 upfront but excludes a parent or sibling can create far more complexity — and cost — down the road when you're filing separate applications or exploring alternative pathways for excluded family members.

The right program protects not just you, but the people who matter most to you.
