Visa-Free Travel Compared: Which CBI Passport Gets You Where in 2026?

November 10, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

Your second passport isn't just a document — it's a global access key. Here's how the top Citizenship by Investment passports stack up.

For high-net-worth individuals evaluating Citizenship by Investment programs, visa-free travel access is often the single most decisive factor. A second passport can mean the difference between a 12-week visa application process and walking straight through immigration with a confident nod.

But not all CBI passports are created equal. The difference between programs can be dozens of countries — and critically, the right countries for your business, lifestyle, and travel patterns.

Let's break down the numbers and, more importantly, the strategic nuances behind them.

The 2026 Visa-Free Scoreboard

| CBI Program | Visa-Free / Visa-on-Arrival Destinations | Notable Access Highlights |

|---|---|---|

| Malta | ~190 | EU/Schengen, UK, US (ESTA eligible), Canada, Japan, Australia, Singapore |

| Portugal Golden Visa | ~190 | EU/Schengen, UK, US (ESTA eligible), Canada, Japan, Australia, Singapore |

| Antigua & Barbuda | ~151 | UK, Schengen, Hong Kong, Singapore, most of Africa & Latin America |

| Vanuatu | ~112 | Schengen, UK, Hong Kong, Russia |

Note: Visa-free counts fluctuate throughout the year as bilateral agreements are signed or suspended. Figures reflect the most current data available as of mid-2026.

Beyond the Numbers: What Actually Matters

Raw visa-free counts make for good headlines, but they can be misleading. A passport with access to 190 countries sounds better than one with 148 — until you realize the 148-country passport is the only one that opens the door to both China and the United States through treaty access.

Here's how to think about it strategically.

🇪🇺 If Your Priority Is Europe

Best picks: Malta or Portugal

This isn't even close. Both are EU member states, meaning your passport grants you the right to live, work, and do business anywhere in the European Union and Schengen Area — not just visit. That includes Germany, France, the Netherlands, Spain, Italy, and 20+ other nations.

The Caribbean passports (St. Kitts, Grenada, Dominica, Antigua) grant visa-free travel to Schengen countries, but stays are capped at 90 days within any 180-day period. There is no right of residence or employment.

The bottom line: If Europe is your operational base or long-term destination, Malta and Portugal are in a league of their own. If you just need easy short-term access for meetings, conferences, and leisure, Caribbean passports serve you well.

🇺🇸 If Your Priority Is the United States

Best pick: Grenada (and it's not just about visa-free travel)

None of the Caribbean CBI passports offer visa-free entry to the United States. Let's be clear about that. However, Grenada holds a unique and enormously valuable advantage: it is the only CBI country with a U.S. E-2 Treaty Investor Visa agreement.

This means Grenadian citizens can apply for the E-2 visa, which allows you to live and work in the U.S. by investing in or operating a business there. For entrepreneurs who want a foothold in the American market without pursuing the far more expensive and time-consuming EB-5 route, this is a game-changer.

Malta and Portugal passport holders can access the U.S. via the ESTA/Visa Waiver Program — no visa interview required for short stays up to 90 days.

The bottom line: For U.S. market access with the right to work and reside, Grenada's E-2 treaty eligibility is unmatched among CBI options. For quick business trips, Malta and Portugal win.

🇨🇳 If Your Priority Is China

Best pick: Grenada

China is notoriously restrictive with visa-free access. Among CBI nations, Grenada stands out with a mutual visa exemption agreement with China allowing stays of up to 30 days. This is extraordinarily rare and particularly valuable for individuals with business interests in mainland China.

Most other CBI passports — including Malta and Portugal — still require a visa for China, though the process is typically straightforward for EU passport holders.

The bottom line: If China is a frequent destination, Grenada is the clear strategic choice.

🇬🇧 If Your Priority Is the United Kingdom

Best picks: All major CBI passports

The UK grants visa-free access to holders of passports from St. Kitts & Nevis, Grenada, Antigua & Barbuda, Dominica, Malta, and Portugal. Stays of up to 6 months are generally permitted for Caribbean passport holders, making this a strong point across the board.

The bottom line: UK access is a near-universal benefit of reputable CBI programs.

🌏 If Your Priority Is Asia-Pacific

Best pick: St. Kitts & Nevis or Malta

For broad Asia-Pacific coverage — including Singapore, Hong Kong, South Korea, Malaysia, the Philippines, and Indonesia — St. Kitts & Nevis offers surprisingly strong coverage among Caribbean programs. Malta and Portugal, as EU passports, naturally cover almost the entire region with visa-free or visa-on-arrival access, including Japan and Australia, which most Caribbean passports do not cover.

The bottom line: For comprehensive Asia-Pacific access including Japan and Australia, EU passports lead. For solid regional coverage at a lower investment threshold, St. Kitts holds its own.

🌍 If Your Priority Is Global Diversification & Geopolitical Hedging

Best pick: St. Kitts & Nevis or Grenada

Both programs have maintained strong diplomatic relationships across geopolitical lines. St. Kitts & Nevis offers visa-free access to Russia, which most EU passports lost following the post-2022 sanctions landscape. For individuals who need to maintain mobility across both Western and non-Western spheres, Caribbean passports offer a flexibility that EU passports sometimes cannot.

The bottom line: A Caribbean passport can complement a Western passport by keeping doors open that geopolitics may have closed.

So, Which Passport Should You Choose?

The honest answer: it depends entirely on your specific situation.

  • Where do you do business?
  • Where does your family need access?
  • Are you optimizing for speed, cost, or breadth of access?
  • Do you need a passport that complements your existing nationality?
  • Are there tax residency implications you need to navigate?

A CBI passport is a strategic asset. Choosing the wrong one is like buying a master key that doesn't open the doors you actually need.
