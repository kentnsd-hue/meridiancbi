Vanuatu Citizenship by Investment: The World's Fastest Passport Program in 2026

September 27, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

Processing time: As little as 30–60 days. No residency requirements. No interview. No travel required.

If speed is your priority, no citizenship by investment program on earth comes close to Vanuatu.

While most CBI programs take 3–6 months (and some stretch well beyond a year), the Republic of Vanuatu has built its entire value proposition around one thing: getting a legitimate, internationally recognized passport into your hands as fast as legally possible.

Here's everything you need to know about the Vanuatu Development Support Program (DSP) in 2026 — the costs, the benefits, the limitations, and whether it's the right fit for your global mobility strategy.

What Is the Vanuatu DSP?

The Development Support Program (DSP) is Vanuatu's official citizenship by investment pathway, launched in 2017 and administered by the Vanuatu Financial Services Commission (VFSC). It allows foreign nationals to obtain full Vanuatu citizenship through a one-time, non-refundable contribution to the country's national development fund.

This isn't a residency permit. It isn't a long-term visa. It is full, irrevocable citizenship — with a passport to match.

Why Vanuatu? The Core Benefits

1. Unmatched Processing Speed

This is the headline, and it's not marketing spin. Approved applications have been processed in as few as 30 days. The standard timeline sits around 45–60 days from submission to passport issuance. No other sovereign citizenship program in the world operates at this pace.

For comparison:

  • St. Kitts & Nevis: 60–90 days (accelerated) / 3–4 months (standard)
  • Grenada: 3–4 months
  • Portugal Golden Visa: 12–18+ months
  • Malta: 12–36 months

If you need a second passport now — for business continuity, geopolitical hedging, or personal security — Vanuatu is the answer.

2. Zero Tax on Global Income

Vanuatu is one of the few remaining zero-income-tax jurisdictions in the world. There is:

  • No personal income tax
  • No corporate tax
  • No capital gains tax
  • No inheritance tax
  • No withholding tax

For entrepreneurs, crypto investors, and anyone structuring international income, Vanuatu citizenship offers a powerful foundation for tax-efficient planning — though the benefits depend heavily on your country of residence and where you actually establish tax residency. Always consult a qualified tax advisor.

3. Visa-Free Travel

The Vanuatu passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to approximately 90–100 destinations, including:

  • United Kingdom (6-month visa-free access)
  • Russia
  • Most of Southeast Asia (Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines, Hong Kong)
  • Schengen Zone — Vanuatu has been in ongoing discussions regarding Schengen visa waiver agreements, though status should be verified at the time of application

While it doesn't match the 150+ destinations of a St. Kitts or Grenada passport, Vanuatu's travel access is meaningful — particularly for individuals prioritizing UK access and Asia-Pacific mobility.

4. No Physical Presence Required

You do not need to visit Vanuatu to apply. You do not need to attend an interview. You do not need to live there — ever. The entire application process is handled remotely through an authorized agent.

5. Dual Citizenship Permitted

Vanuatu fully recognizes dual (and multiple) citizenship. You are not required to renounce your existing nationality.

Investment Requirements: What Does It Cost?

The Vanuatu DSP is a donation-based program. There is no real estate option — you make a direct, non-refundable contribution to the government.

| Applicant Type | Government Contribution |

|---|---|

| Single applicant | ~USD $130,000 |

| Married couple | ~USD $150,000 |

| Family of 4 (couple + 2 dependents) | ~USD $180,000 |

Note: Fees are subject to change. Additional due diligence fees, legal fees, and processing charges apply. Total all-in costs typically range from $150,000–$230,000+ depending on family size and advisory fees.

These figures make Vanuatu competitive with — and in some cases more affordable than — Caribbean programs when speed and tax structure are factored in.

Eligibility Criteria

Vanuatu's requirements are straightforward:

  • Age: 18 or older (dependents can be included)
  • Clean criminal record: No serious criminal history
  • Source of funds: Demonstrable legitimate source of wealth
  • Health: No communicable diseases that pose a public health risk
  • Net worth: Generally expected to demonstrate a minimum net worth (varies, but typically USD $250,000+)

Dependents can include:

  • Spouse
  • Children under 25
  • Parents over 50 (in some cases)

The Application Process: Step by Step

Step 1 — Initial Consultation & Pre-Qualification (Week 1)

Work with an authorized advisory firm to assess eligibility, gather documents, and determine the optimal application structure.

Step 2 — Document Preparation (Weeks 1–2)

Compile required documentation: certified passport copies, police clearance certificates, bank references, medical certificate, proof of source of funds, and completed application forms.

Step 3 — Submission & Due Diligence (Weeks 2–4)

Your application is submitted to the VFSC. A thorough due diligence process is conducted, including international database checks and background verification.

Step 4 — Approval in Principle (Weeks 4–6)

Upon passing due diligence, you receive an approval in principle. Government contribution fees are payable at this stage.

Step 5 — Citizenship & Passport Issuance (Weeks 5–8)

Citizenship certificate and passport are issued. Documents can be couriered to you or collected through your advisory firm.

Total timeline: 30–60 days in most cases.

Important Considerations & Limitations

No program is perfect, and transparency matters. Here's what you should weigh carefully:

Travel Access Is More Limited Than Caribbean Programs

With ~90–100 destinations, the Vanuatu passport doesn't open the same doors as Grenada (which also provides a pathway to the US E-2 visa) or St. Kitts. If Schengen access and US optionality are priorities, a Caribbean program may serve you better — either alone or as a complement.

Reputation & Perception

Vanuatu's CBI program has faced scrutiny in the past over due diligence standards. In recent years, the government has taken significant steps to tighten vetting procedures and align with international compliance standards. However, perception matters — particularly if you're a public-facing executive or politically exposed person. This is worth discussing with your advisor.

No Real Estate Option

Unlike Portugal, Grenada, or St. Kitts, there is no investment recovery mechanism. The contribution is a sunk cost. You're paying purely for citizenship and the passport — not an asset.

Renunciation from Certain Countries

While Vanuatu allows dual citizenship, your home country may not. Some nations require renunciation of original citizenship if you acquire a second. Know your obligations before applying.

Who Is the Vanuatu Passport Best For?

Based on the profile of applicants we advise, Vanuatu tends to be the strongest fit for:

  • Crypto investors and DeFi founders seeking a zero-tax jurisdiction with fast processing
  • Business owners in unstable regions who need an immediate backup passport for personal security
  • Frequent Asia-Pacific travelers who want streamlined access across the region
  • Individuals who already hold a strong passport (US, EU, UK) and want a complementary tax-efficient citizenship
  • Anyone who needs a second passport in under 60 days — full stop

The Bottom Line

Vanuatu won't give you the strongest passport on the planet. It won't unlock the US E-2 visa. And it requires a non-recoverable financial commitment.

But nothing else on the market delivers full citizenship this fast, with zero tax obligations and zero residency requirements.

For the right applicant — someone who values speed, tax efficiency, and Asia-Pacific access — Vanuatu is an extraordinarily powerful tool in a global mobility portfolio. And for those who already hold a primary passport from a strong jurisdiction, it serves as an ideal complement: a clean, efficient second citizenship with no strings attached.
