Vanuatu Citizenship by Investment: The World's Fastest Passport Program in 2026

September 04, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

Processing time: as little as 30–60 days. No interview. No residency requirement. No visit required.

If speed is your priority, no CBI program on earth competes with Vanuatu.

While programs in the Caribbean typically process applications in 3–6 months, and European programs can stretch well beyond a year, the Republic of Vanuatu has carved out an extraordinary niche: full, legal citizenship — and a passport in your hand — in as little as one to two months.

Here's everything high-net-worth individuals, entrepreneurs, and global investors need to know about the Vanuatu Development Support Program (DSP) in 2026.

What Is the Vanuatu DSP?

The Development Support Program (DSP) is Vanuatu's official citizenship-by-investment pathway, authorized under the Citizenship Act and administered by the Vanuatu Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU) and the Citizenship Commission.

Launched in 2017, the program grants irrevocable citizenship to qualifying applicants and their families in exchange for a non-refundable economic contribution to the country's development fund.

It is not a residency program. It is not a visa. It is full, constitutional citizenship — with a passport issued by the Vanuatu government.

Why Vanuatu? The Key Advantages

1. Unmatched Speed

Most approved applicants receive citizenship within 30–60 days of submitting a complete application. In some cases, processing has been completed in under 30 days. No other legitimate CBI program comes close to this timeline.

2. Visa-Free Travel to 90+ Countries

The Vanuatu passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to approximately 90–100 destinations, including:

  • United Kingdom (6 months visa-free)
  • Schengen Zone (90 days under ETIAS starting 2026)
  • Hong Kong & Singapore
  • Russia
  • Most of the Caribbean, Africa, and the Pacific Islands

For entrepreneurs doing business across Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Commonwealth, this is a meaningful mobility upgrade.

3. Zero Personal Income Tax

Vanuatu levies no personal income tax, no capital gains tax, no inheritance tax, and no wealth tax. For crypto holders, digital entrepreneurs, and investors managing global portfolios, establishing Vanuatu citizenship can be a foundational element of a legitimate international tax strategy.

4. No Physical Residency Requirement

You do not need to live in Vanuatu. You do not need to visit Vanuatu. You do not even need to attend an interview. The entire application can be processed remotely through an authorized agent.

5. Dual Citizenship Permitted

Vanuatu fully recognizes dual (and multiple) citizenship. You do not need to renounce your existing nationality.

6. Commonwealth Membership

Vanuatu is a member of the Commonwealth of Nations, which provides certain diplomatic, educational, and business advantages across 56 member states.

Investment Requirements & Costs

The Vanuatu DSP is structured as a straightforward government contribution model:

| Applicant Type | Government Contribution (USD) |

|---|---|

| Single Applicant | ~$130,000 |

| Couple | ~$150,000 |

| Family of 4 (couple + 2 children) | ~$180,000 |

| Each Additional Dependent | ~$15,000–$25,000 |

Note: Fees are subject to change. Additional due diligence fees, legal fees, and agent processing fees apply. Contact us for a precise, current quote based on your family composition.

These are non-refundable contributions — not investments with a return. The funds go directly toward Vanuatu's national development priorities, including infrastructure, education, and disaster resilience.

Eligibility Requirements

The program is designed to be accessible, but applicants must meet basic criteria:

  • Age: 18 years or older
  • Clean criminal record: No serious criminal history
  • Source of funds: Ability to demonstrate legitimate source of wealth
  • Good health: Basic health declaration (no major medical exam required)
  • Due diligence clearance: Background checks conducted through international databases

Dependents who can be included:

  • Spouse
  • Children under 25
  • Parents over 50 (in some cases)

Common Concerns — Addressed Honestly

"Is Vanuatu citizenship legitimate?"

Yes. The DSP is codified in Vanuatu's national law and administered by the government's Citizenship Commission. Vanuatu passports are internationally recognized travel documents.

"Will it affect my current citizenship?"

In most cases, no. Vanuatu allows dual citizenship. However, you should verify whether your current country permits dual nationality. We can advise on this during your consultation.

"Is the passport strong enough to be useful?"

It depends on your goals. If you need US or Canada visa-free access, Vanuatu won't provide that. But for UK, Europe (Schengen), Asia-Pacific, and Commonwealth access — combined with zero income tax and rapid processing — it is one of the most strategically valuable passports available at this price point.

"What about the EU's scrutiny of CBI programs?"

Vanuatu is not an EU member state, so it falls outside the European Commission's direct regulatory actions against EU-based CBI programs (like those that shut down Cyprus and Bulgaria's schemes). However, Vanuatu has been proactive about strengthening its due diligence standards in recent years to maintain international credibility.
