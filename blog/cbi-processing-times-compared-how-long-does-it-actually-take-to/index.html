CBI Processing Times Compared: How Long Does It Actually Take to Get a Second Passport in 2026?

November 14, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

By Meridian Advisory | Updated June 2026

One of the first questions every client asks us is deceptively simple: "How long will this take?"

It's the right question. Whether you're seeking a second citizenship to unlock visa-free travel, protect your family's future, or diversify your global footprint, timing matters. A deal-closing trip to the UK next quarter, a planned relocation, a shifting tax landscape — the clock is always ticking.

The problem? Most firms give you vague answers. "Three to six months, depending on the program." That's not good enough.

At Meridian Advisory, we believe in radical transparency. So here's our comprehensive, data-backed breakdown of CBI and residency-by-investment processing times as they stand in 2026 — along with the real-world factors that speed things up or slow them down.

Let's break each one down.

🇰🇳 St. Kitts & Nevis: The Gold Standard for Speed

Average timeline: 60–90 days

Accelerated Processing Option (APO): ~45 days

St. Kitts & Nevis operates the world's oldest CBI program, established in 1984, and it shows. Their Citizenship by Investment Unit (CIU) has processed tens of thousands of applications over four decades, and the machinery is exceptionally well-oiled.

What the timeline actually looks like:

  • Weeks 1–2: Document collection, notarization, and application preparation
  • Weeks 3–8: Due diligence and government processing
  • Weeks 9–12: Approval, passport issuance, and delivery

The Accelerated Processing Option adds a premium fee but compresses the government review phase significantly. For clients with urgent travel or business needs, this is often worth every dollar.

What can slow things down: Complex corporate structures, applicants with ties to high-risk jurisdictions, incomplete documentation (the number one delay we see across every program).

Visa-free access: 150+ countries, including the UK, EU Schengen zone, and Singapore.

> 💡 Meridian Advisory insight: St. Kitts is our most recommended program for clients who need speed without sacrificing passport strength. The combination of processing time, visa-free access, and program maturity is unmatched in the Caribbean.

🇬🇩 Grenada: The Strategic Pick (Especially for U.S.-Bound Investors)

Average timeline: 90–120 days

Grenada consistently processes applications in the three-to-four-month range. It's not the fastest, but it's far from slow — and Grenada offers something no other Caribbean CBI program does: eligibility for the U.S. E-2 Treaty Investor Visa.

That single advantage makes Grenada the program of choice for entrepreneurs and investors who want a path to living and working in the United States without going through the notoriously backlogged EB-5 process.

What the timeline actually looks like:

  • Weeks 1–3: Application preparation and submission
  • Weeks 4–12: Government due diligence (conducted by multiple international agencies)
  • Weeks 13–16: Approval notification, oath of allegiance, and passport issuance

What can slow things down: Grenada's due diligence is thorough. Applicants with complex international business portfolios should expect to sit at the longer end of the range. This isn't a negative — it's part of what keeps the Grenada passport strong and respected internationally.

> 💡 Meridian Advisory insight: If the U.S. market is part of your business or lifestyle strategy, Grenada is almost certainly your best first move. We've helped multiple clients go from Grenada application to E-2 visa approval in under 18 months total.

🇩🇲 Dominica: Affordable and Reliable

Average timeline: 90–120 days

Dominica offers one of the most cost-effective CBI programs in the world, and the processing timeline mirrors Grenada's. The government has invested heavily in modernizing its CIU in recent years, and it shows in the consistency of processing times.

Minimum investment: Starting at $100,000 (single applicant, government fund contribution) — the lowest entry point of any major CBI program.

Best for: Cost-conscious investors who prioritize value. Dominica's passport offers visa-free access to 140+ countries, including the UK, EU Schengen area, and Singapore.

What can slow things down: The same factors as other Caribbean programs — documentation gaps and complex backgrounds.

🇦🇬 Antigua & Barbuda: Family-Friendly Processing

Average timeline: 90–120 days

Antigua's program is particularly attractive for families, as it allows the inclusion of dependent children up to age 30 and dependent parents over 55. Processing times are competitive with Grenada and Dominica.

Notable requirement: Antigua requires at least five days of physical presence in the country within the first five years. It's minimal, but it's worth noting — and frankly, spending a week in Antigua is hardly a hardship.

Best for: Larger families seeking to bring multiple generations under a single application.

🇻🇺 Vanuatu: The Fastest Passport on Earth

Average timeline: 30–60 days

If pure speed is your priority, Vanuatu is in a class of its own. The Development Support Program (DSP) has been consistently delivering approved citizenships in as little as 30 days from submission.

The trade-off: Vanuatu's passport provides visa-free access to approximately 90+ countries, including the UK and all EU Schengen states — but it lacks some of the destinations available to Caribbean program holders. Additionally, Vanuatu's program has faced occasional scrutiny from international watchdogs, though the government has tightened due diligence standards considerably in recent years.

Best for: Clients who need a second citizenship immediately — whether for urgent travel, geopolitical hedging, or as a bridge while a longer-term program (like Malta or Portugal) is processed.

> 💡 Meridian Advisory insight: We sometimes recommend a "two-passport strategy" — Vanuatu for immediate coverage, paired with a Malta or Portugal application for long-term premium access. Rachel can walk you through whether this makes sense for your situation.

🇲🇹 Malta: The Premium European Passport

Average timeline: 12–14 months

Malta's program — formally the Malta Exceptional Investor Naturalisation (MEIN) — is the only program in the European Union that leads directly to citizenship. And that exclusivity comes with a correspondingly rigorous and lengthy process.

What the timeline actually looks like:

  • Months 1–2: Application preparation (extensive documentation)
  • Months 3–10: Multi-layered due diligence, including Europol and Interpol checks
  • Months 11–12: Residency requirement verification (12 months for the standard track, 36 months for the reduced-contribution track)
  • Months 12–14: Citizenship approval, oath of allegiance, passport issuance

Minimum investment: Approximately €690,000–€890,000+ (combination of contribution, real estate, and charitable donation). This is the most expensive CBI program in the world — but you're getting arguably the world's most powerful passport.

Visa-free access: 170+ countries, plus the right to live and work anywhere in the EU.

What can slow things down: Malta's due diligence is the most exhaustive in the industry. This is by design. The Maltese government limits approvals to a small number per year, and rejection rates are meaningfully higher than Caribbean programs.

Best for: Ultra-high-net-worth individuals who want full EU citizenship and are willing to invest the time and capital to obtain it.

> 💡 Meridian Advisory insight: Malta is a long game, but it's the ultimate prize in CBI. We recommend starting the application as early as possible and pairing it with a faster Caribbean program if you need immediate mobility.

🇵🇹 Portugal Golden Visa: The Long Road Worth Traveling

Average timeline: 12–18 months (initial residency permit) → 5 years (citizenship eligibility)

Portugal's Golden Visa is technically a residency-by-investment program, not a citizenship program. But it leads to one of the most valuable citizenships in the world — full Portuguese (and therefore EU) citizenship — after five years.

Key 2026 update: Portugal has revised its Golden Visa program to focus on approved investment funds, venture capital, and other qualifying categories after removing direct real estate purchases in major urban areas. The program remains one of the most popular residency-by-investment pathways in Europe.

What the timeline actually looks like:

  • Months 1–3: Investment selection, fund subscription, and application preparation
  • Months 4–14: SEF/AIMA processing and biometrics appointment
  • Months 14–18: Initial residency permit issued
  • Years 2–5: Biennial renewals (minimal physical presence required — just 7 days per year)
  • Year 5: Citizenship application (requires basic Portuguese language test, A2 level)

Best for: Investors with a 5-year horizon who want EU citizenship, access to Portugal's favorable NHR tax regime, and a lifestyle foothold in one of Europe's most desirable countries.

> 💡 Meridian Advisory insight: Portugal is our top recommendation for clients who aren't in a rush and want the strongest possible long-term outcome. The combination of EU citizenship, Portugal's quality of life, and the relatively low physical presence requirements makes this program exceptional.

🇹🇷 Türkiye: The Underrated Middle Ground

Average timeline: 90–180 days

Türkiye's CBI program — typically through a $400,000 real estate investment — offers a unique combination of a large, dynamic economy, strategic geographic location, and surprisingly strong passport (visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to 110+ countries).

The wide processing range explained: Türkiye's timeline is more variable than Caribbean programs because it involves multiple government agencies (land registry, immigration directorate, and the presidency's investment office). The real estate transaction itself can add weeks depending on property selection and transfer logistics.

Best for: Investors who want tangible real estate assets alongside their citizenship, and those with business interests in the Middle East, Central Asia, or Africa where Turkish diplomatic presence provides advantages.

The Hidden Factor: Your Preparation

Here's what most CBI firms won't tell you: the single biggest variable in processing time is how well your application is prepared before submission.

Across every program, we see the same pattern. Incomplete applications get sent back. Missing apostilles add weeks. Poorly structured source-of-funds documentation triggers additional due diligence rounds. A family of four where one dependent's documents are missing holds up the entire file.

At Meridian Advisory, we typically spend 2–4 weeks on pre-submission preparation — and we consider it the highest-ROI phase of the entire process. A bulletproof application submitted on Day 1 will almost always beat a hastily filed one that hits the queue two weeks earlier.

Which Program Is Right for You?

The "best" program doesn't exist in a vacuum. It depends on your:

  • Timeline: Do you need a passport in 60 days or can you plan over 5 years?
  • Budget: Are you working with $100K or $1M+?
  • Travel needs: Which specific countries do you need visa-free access to?
  • Tax strategy: Are you restructuring your tax residency?
  • Family situation: How many dependents need to be included?
  • Business goals: Do you need U.S. E-2 eligibility? EU market access?
