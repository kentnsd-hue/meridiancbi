CBI Processing Times Compared: How Long Does It Actually Take to Get a Second Passport in 2026?

November 26, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

Published by Meridian Advisory | meridiancbi.com | Updated: June 2026

One of the first questions every prospective applicant asks is deceptively simple: "How long will this take?"

It's also one of the most misunderstood topics in the citizenship by investment world. Government websites quote one timeline. Agents quote another. Reddit threads paint yet another picture entirely.

At Meridian Advisory, we believe in transparency. So we've put together this comprehensive breakdown of real-world processing times across the most popular CBI and residency-by-investment programs in 2026 — based on actual case data, not marketing brochures.

\Realistic timelines include document preparation, due diligence, government processing, and passport issuance — the full end-to-end journey from engagement to passport in hand.*

Breaking It Down: Program by Program

🇰🇳 St. Kitts & Nevis — The Gold Standard for Speed and Prestige

Investment minimum: $250,000 (real estate) or $250,000 (Sustainable Island State Contribution — SISC)

Timeline: 60–120 days | Accelerated: 45–60 days

St. Kitts remains the flagship CBI program for a reason. Established in 1984, it's the oldest program in the world, and the government has invested heavily in streamlining its processing infrastructure.

The Accelerated Application Process (AAP) is a game-changer for applicants who need urgency. For an additional government fee, applications can be fast-tracked to as little as 45 days from submission to approval. We've seen cases clear in under 50 days when documentation was airtight from day one.

What affects timing:

  • Completeness of your document package at submission
  • Complexity of your source-of-funds documentation
  • Number of dependents on the application
  • Whether enhanced due diligence is triggered

Meridian's take: If speed is your top priority and you want a well-established, globally respected passport (visa-free access to 150+ destinations), St. Kitts with the AAP is hard to beat in 2026.

🇬🇩 Grenada — The Strategic Choice

Investment minimum: $235,000 (National Transformation Fund) or $270,000 (real estate)

Timeline: 90–150 days

Grenada's processing times are slightly longer than St. Kitts, but the program offers something no other Caribbean CBI does: eligibility for the U.S. E-2 Treaty Investor Visa. This single advantage makes Grenada the top choice for entrepreneurs and investors who want a pathway to live and work in the United States.

The Grenadian government conducts thorough due diligence, and there's no formal expedited track. Applications move through a structured review process that typically takes 3–5 months from submission.

What affects timing:

  • The E-2 angle attracts applicants with more complex business structures, which can extend due diligence
  • Real estate applications may take slightly longer due to purchase verification
  • Family applications with multiple dependents add processing layers

Meridian's take: Don't choose Grenada for speed — choose it for strategy. If U.S. market access is part of your five-year plan, the extra 30–60 days of processing is a rounding error compared to the value of E-2 eligibility.

🇩🇲 Dominica — The Budget-Friendly Workhorse

Investment minimum: $200,000 (Economic Diversification Fund) or $200,000 (real estate)

Timeline: 90–120 days

Dominica consistently offers one of the most cost-effective CBI programs in the world. Processing times have improved significantly over the past two years, with the government investing in digital infrastructure and additional due diligence staff.

While an expedited option exists in limited form, it's not as formalized or reliable as St. Kitts' AAP. Standard applications typically clear in 3–4 months.

Meridian's take: Dominica is the smart choice for cost-conscious applicants who don't need the absolute fastest timeline but want a legitimate, well-regarded second passport without overpaying.

🇦🇬 Antigua & Barbuda — The Family-Friendly Option

Investment minimum: $230,000 (National Development Fund) or $300,000 (real estate)

Timeline: 90–150 days

Antigua stands out for its generous family inclusion policies — you can include parents, grandparents, and siblings under certain conditions. The government holds regular "joint sittings" of the CBI committee, which can accelerate batch approvals.

However, the broader family inclusion means more individuals undergoing due diligence, which can extend overall timelines for larger family applications.

Meridian's take: Ideal for applicants looking to secure citizenship for extended family in a single application. Budget 4–5 months for families with more than four dependents.

🇻🇺 Vanuatu — The Speed King

Investment minimum: ~$130,000 (Development Support Program)

Timeline: 45–90 days

Vanuatu is, hands down, the fastest CBI program in the world. Some applications have been approved in as few as 30 days. The program operates with remarkable efficiency, and the government has made speed a core selling point.

The trade-off? The Vanuatu passport offers visa-free access to roughly 95+ destinations — respectable, but notably fewer than the Caribbean programs or European options. There have also been periodic concerns about the program's international perception, though the government has taken steps to strengthen due diligence standards.

Meridian's take: If absolute speed is the priority and you need a second passport now, Vanuatu delivers. But we always encourage clients to weigh passport strength and long-term program stability alongside processing speed.

🇲🇹 Malta — The European Powerhouse

Investment minimum: €690,000+ (contribution, property, and philanthropy combined)

Timeline: 14–18 months

Malta's Exceptional Investor Naturalisation (MEIN) program is in a league of its own. It's the only current program that grants direct citizenship in an EU member state — and an EU passport is, by virtually every measure, the most powerful travel and residency document you can hold.

The price of that power is time and cost. Malta conducts the most rigorous due diligence in the CBI world, involving four-tier background checks and a mandatory 12-month (or 36-month, depending on investment level) residency period before naturalisation.

What affects timing:

  • The mandatory residency period is the single biggest factor
  • Due diligence is exhaustive and cannot be rushed
  • Document authentication and apostille requirements for non-EU nationals

Meridian's take: Malta is not a quick fix — it's a long-term strategic asset. If you can afford the investment and the wait, an EU passport fundamentally changes your global positioning. This is the program we recommend to clients thinking in decades, not months.

🇵🇹 Portugal Golden Visa — The Residency Pathway

Investment minimum: €500,000 (fund subscription, post-2023 restructuring)

Timeline: 18–30+ months (to residency card) | 5+ years to citizenship

Portugal's Golden Visa has undergone significant restructuring in recent years, with real estate removed as a qualifying investment category for most applicants. The program now primarily channels investments into qualifying funds.

Processing times have been a pain point. AIMA (the successor agency to SEF) has faced substantial backlogs, and while the government has committed to reducing wait times, the reality on the ground in 2026 is that applicants should expect 18–30 months to receive their initial residency card.

The citizenship pathway requires five years of legal residency, basic Portuguese language proficiency (A2 level), and maintained investment throughout.

What affects timing:

  • AIMA processing backlogs remain the primary bottleneck
  • Biometric appointment availability varies by region
  • Fund subscription verification adds an administrative layer

Meridian's take: Portugal is a phenomenal long-term play — EU residency, a path to one of the world's strongest passports, and access to Portugal's favorable NHR 2.0 tax regime. But it requires patience and a genuine five-to-seven-year horizon. We help clients set realistic expectations from day one.

🇹🇷 Türkiye — The Underrated Contender

Investment minimum: $400,000 (real estate)

Timeline: 120–180 days

Türkiye's CBI program offers a compelling blend of a G20 passport, a large and dynamic domestic market, and real estate investment in a country with strong rental yield potential. Processing typically runs 4–6 months, though bureaucratic variability can push some cases longer.

Meridian's take: Often overlooked in favor of Caribbean programs, Türkiye offers tangible real estate value alongside citizenship. The passport provides visa-free access to 110+ countries and is steadily improving in global rankings.

The Hidden Variable: Your Preparation

Here's what most articles won't tell you: the single biggest factor in your processing time is not the government — it's you.

Specifically, it's how quickly and completely you can produce:

  • Source of funds documentation (bank statements, business financials, tax returns)
  • Certified translations of all civil documents
  • Apostilled or authenticated birth certificates, marriage certificates, police clearances
  • Professional reference letters and character declarations
  • Medical examinations from approved providers

At Meridian Advisory, we've seen the difference between a well-prepared application and a poorly prepared one amount to 60–90 days of additional processing time. That's not the government's fault — it's avoidable delay caused by incomplete submissions, rejected documents, and back-and-forth requests for additional information.

Our role is to eliminate that friction. We prepare your entire application package before submission, ensuring it meets every requirement on first review.
