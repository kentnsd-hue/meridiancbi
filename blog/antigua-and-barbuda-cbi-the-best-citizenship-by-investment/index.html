Antigua and Barbuda CBI: The Best Citizenship by Investment Program for Large Families in 2026

October 04, 2026 | Meridian Advisory

Why this twin-island nation remains the go-to choice for families seeking second citizenship together — without breaking the bank.

Introduction: When Your Family Is Your Greatest Asset

For high-net-worth individuals exploring second citizenship, one question comes up more often than you might expect:

"Can I include my entire family?"

It's a fair question. Most CBI programs charge steep additional fees for each dependent, making the total cost balloon quickly for families with multiple children, aging parents, or adult dependents still building their careers. What starts as a $100K investment can quietly become a $250K+ commitment once you add everyone you care about.

This is exactly where Antigua and Barbuda stands apart from every other program on the market.

In 2026, Antigua's Citizenship by Investment Programme remains the single most family-friendly CBI option in the world — and it's not even close. Here's why.

What Makes Antigua & Barbuda Uniquely Family-Friendly?

1. The Most Generous Dependent Policy in Any CBI Program

Antigua and Barbuda allows applicants to include an exceptionally wide range of family members on a single application:

  • Spouse
  • Children under 30 (including adult children enrolled in higher education)
  • Parents and grandparents aged 55+ of both the main applicant and the spouse
  • Siblings of the main applicant — unmarried, with no children, and under age 30

Read that last one again. Siblings. No other major CBI program allows you to include brothers or sisters on the same application. For families who want to move as a unit — or simply want the shared security of a second passport — this is a game-changer.

2. A Pricing Structure That Rewards Larger Families

Here's where the math gets compelling. Under the National Development Fund (NDF) donation route, the current contribution schedule in 2026 is structured as follows:

| Family Size | NDF Contribution |

|---|---|

| Single applicant | $230,000 |

| Family of up to 4 | $230,000 |

| Family of 5+ | $230,000 + $15,000 per additional dependent |

Compare this to most Caribbean programs, where each additional dependent can add $25,000–$50,000 to your total outlay. For a family of six or seven, Antigua often comes in tens of thousands of dollars cheaper than competing programs — while offering a broader definition of who qualifies as a dependent in the first place.

> The bottom line: A family of 7 in Antigua could pay significantly less than a family of 4 in some competing jurisdictions. The per-person cost drops dramatically as your family size increases.

3. The Real Estate Route Works for Families Too

For families who prefer an investment with tangible asset value, Antigua offers a real estate option starting at $300,000 in an approved development. This investment must be held for a minimum of five years.

What makes this particularly attractive for large families:

  • The real estate investment amount stays the same regardless of family size — you're only adjusting government and processing fees per dependent, not the core investment.
  • Some approved projects include resort properties that generate rental income, offsetting the holding cost.
  • After the five-year hold, the property can be resold, potentially recovering a substantial portion (or all) of the investment.

For a patriarch or matriarch looking to secure passports for a large multi-generational household, the real estate route often delivers the best overall value per family member of any CBI program globally.

Beyond Pricing: Why Antigua Makes Sense for Families in 2026

Cost matters, but it's not everything. Here's what else makes Antigua & Barbuda stand out for family-oriented applicants.

🌍 Visa-Free Travel to 150+ Destinations

An Antigua and Barbuda passport provides visa-free or visa-on-arrival access to over 150 countries and territories, including:

  • United Kingdom
  • Schengen Zone (Europe)
  • Hong Kong
  • Singapore
  • All Caribbean and most Latin American nations

For families who travel frequently — whether for business, education, or leisure — this level of mobility is transformative. Parents can attend conferences in London while their university-aged children study across Europe, all on the same passport.

🏫 Education and Opportunity

Antigua's CBI program has historically attracted families partly because of the access it unlocks for the next generation. A Commonwealth passport can simplify university admissions processes in the UK, Canada, and other Commonwealth nations, and it removes the visa barriers that can derail a student's plans at the worst possible moment.

☀️ An Actual Place You'd Want to Visit

Unlike some CBI jurisdictions that exist mostly on paper for passport holders, Antigua and Barbuda is a genuinely world-class destination. With 365 beaches (yes, one for every day of the year), a thriving sailing culture, and a growing expat community, many families find themselves actually spending time on the islands — turning a financial decision into a lifestyle upgrade.

🕐 Processing Timeline

Current processing times in 2026 run approximately 3 to 6 months from submission of a complete application to passport issuance. For families, this is particularly meaningful: everyone gets approved and receives their citizenship simultaneously, rather than navigating staggered timelines.

🏠 Short-Term Residency Requirement

Antigua does require that citizens spend a minimum of 5 days in the country within the first five years of obtaining citizenship. That's five days total, not annually. For a family, this essentially amounts to one vacation — hardly a burden, and for many, an eagerly anticipated trip.

The comparison makes one thing clear: if your priority is bringing the most family members under one application at the lowest per-person cost, Antigua is the leader.

Grenada does hold a unique advantage with its US E-2 Investor Visa treaty — if access to the United States is a top priority, that's worth a separate conversation. But for pure family inclusion and cost efficiency, Antigua remains unmatched.

Who Is This Program Ideal For?

Based on the families we work with at Meridian Advisory, the Antigua & Barbuda CBI program is the strongest fit for:

  • Multi-generational families who want parents, grandparents, and adult children on one application
  • Business owners with young-adult siblings who want to provide global mobility to their brothers or sisters
  • Families of 5 or more looking for the best cost-per-passport ratio
  • Entrepreneurs and investors who want a Plan B for their entire household — not just themselves
  • Families in regions with restricted passport mobility seeking immediate access to Europe, the UK, and the broader Commonwealth

Common Questions We Hear

"Do all family members need to attend an interview?"

No. The application process is handled through an authorized agent. No interview is required, and family members do not need to travel to Antigua during the application process.

"Can I add family members later?"

Yes. Antigua allows you to add dependents after your initial approval — for example, a newborn child or a newly married spouse. Additional government fees apply.

"What about dual citizenship? Will my home country find out?"

Antigua and Barbuda fully recognizes dual citizenship. Whether your home country does is a separate legal question we help clients navigate during the consultation process. Meridian Advisory works with legal counsel across multiple jurisdictions to ensure full compliance.

"Is there a minimum net worth requirement?"

There is no publicly stated minimum net worth threshold to apply. However, applicants must demonstrate that their investment funds are from a legitimate, legal source, and the due diligence process is rigorous. In practice, this program serves individuals and families with meaningful financial resources.

The Risks and Realities: What You Should Know

No advisory firm worth its reputation will tell you CBI is without nuance. Here's what to keep in mind:

  • Regulatory landscape: The global environment for CBI programs continues to evolve. The EU has pressured Caribbean nations on due diligence standards, and programs can change terms with relatively short notice. Applying sooner rather than later protects you against potential price increases or policy tightening.
  • Due diligence is real: Antigua's CBI Unit conducts thorough background checks on all applicants and adult dependents. A clean legal and financial history is non-negotiable.
  • It's not a tax strategy by itself: Citizenship does not automatically change your tax residency. We always recommend working with qualified international tax counsel alongside the CBI process.
